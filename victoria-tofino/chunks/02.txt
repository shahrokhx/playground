<section class="panel" id="packing"><div class="planhead"><div class="content"><div class="eyebrow">The difference between cozy and miserable</div><h1>Packing + road + drone</h1><p>Fall Vancouver Island rewards the right shell, shoes and driving setup.</p></div></div><div class="content">
  <div class="grid">
    <div class="card span6"><h3>Wear / pack</h3><ul><li><b>Waterproof shell with hood</b> — more important than a heavy coat.</li><li>Mid-layer fleece or wool sweater.</li><li>Packable insulated jacket for evenings.</li><li><b>Waterproof shoes/boots</b> with traction + spare socks.</li><li>Quick-dry pants; avoid relying on denim for wet trail days.</li><li>Beanie + light gloves in November.</li><li>Small umbrella for Victoria; <b>not</b> your main Tofino solution in wind.</li><li>Camera rain cover, lens cloths, dry bag/zip pouches, power bank.</li><li>Small tripod for Victoria blue hour.</li></ul></div>
    <div class="card span6"><h3>Road / car</h3><ul><li><b>Highway 4 to Tofino requires winter tires or chains Oct 1–Mar 31.</b> Explicitly confirm the rental has compliant tires.</li><li>Victoria→Tofino is roughly <b>4h20</b> without meaningful stops; Swartz Bay→Tofino ~4h40.</li><li>Nanaimo Departure Bay→Tofino is just under <b>3h</b>.</li><li>Cathedral Grove makes the drive better; budget 45–60 min there.</li><li>In November, plan the Highway 4 segment mainly in daylight.</li><li>Reserve car ferries in advance when using YVR routing.</li></ul></div>
  </div>
  <div class="callout warn"><strong>Drone reality:</strong> this is much less drone-friendly than it looks on Instagram. Pacific Rim National Park Reserve prohibits recreational drone use without authorization. BC Parks also prohibits drone use without permission, so Cathedral Grove is out. Tofino itself has restrictions because of Tofino Harbour Water Aerodrome and Tofino–Long Beach Airport. Victoria’s harbour/airport environment is also complex.</div>
  <div class="grid"><div class="card span8"><h3>What I’d do with the drone</h3><p>Bring it only if you’re comfortable treating it as an <b>optional</b> tool, not a core activity. Before any flight, check Transport Canada’s live drone map, the launch property’s rules, nearby aerodromes, and park boundaries. For a sub-250g microdrone, registration/pilot certification is generally not required, but safe/legal operation rules still apply.</p><div class="btnrow"><a class="btn" target="_blank" href="https://nrc.canada.ca/en/drone-tool/">Transport Canada / NRC drone map ↗</a><a class="btn secondary" target="_blank" href="https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/securite-safety/reglements-regulations">Pacific Rim rules ↗</a><a class="btn secondary" target="_blank" href="https://bcparks.ca/plan-your-trip/visit-responsibly/drones/">BC Parks drone rules ↗</a></div></div><div class="card span4"><h3>Camera > drone here</h3><p>The strongest visuals — giant trees, rainforest boardwalks, storm surf, harbour lights, couple photos on wet sand — are actually better suited to a normal camera/phone anyway.</p></div></div>
